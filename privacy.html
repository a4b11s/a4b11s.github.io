Privacy Policy

Engineer's Toolbox for Android · Effective 6 July 2026

Short version: the app collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. The app requests no permissions and does not use the internet.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter — calculator values, favorites, recently used tools, and calculation history — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It never leaves it through us: we run no servers and receive no data.

Android backup

If you have Android Backup enabled on your device, the operating system may include the app's data in your device backup (for example, to restore your favorites on a new phone). That backup is managed by Android and your Google account under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Google Play

Installing and updating the app is handled by Google Play, which may process data as described in its own privacy policy. This is outside our control.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the app changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated here before that version is released, and the change will be reflected in the app's Play Store data safety section.
